A database is an organized system for storing and managing data. Instead of just keeping information in random files, it uses tables or other structures to arrange it in a clear way. This makes it easy to add, update, and find specific pieces of information quickly and efficiently.

---
A Database Management System, or DBMS, is software that helps you create, manage, and interact with a database. It provides tools for adding, updating, and querying data, along with ensuring security, integrity, and backups. Popular examples are MySQL, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB.
