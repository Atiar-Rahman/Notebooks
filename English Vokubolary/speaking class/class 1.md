## Read the whole introduction naturally

> Hi, my name is Atiar Rahman. I come from Bangladesh. I live in Dhaka. I'm a student. There are five people in my family. They are my father, my mother, my brother, my sister, and me. I'm a student at XYZ College. My major is Computer Science. My favorite subject is English. My hobbies are reading books, playing football, and listening to music. In my free time, I also enjoy watching movies. I don't like smoking. My favorite food is biryani. I like action movies. My favorite singer is Arijit Singh. I study English because I want to improve my English. I would like to be a doctor.

### Speaking Tip

Practice this introduction **5–10 times every day**. Try to speak **without looking at the page**, then record yourself and listen for pronunciation and fluency. This is an effective way to build confidence in spoken English.

## Parts of Speech মনে রাখার সহজ নিয়ম

* **Noun (N)** → ব্যক্তি, বস্তু, স্থান, ধারণার নাম।
* **Pronoun (Pro)** → Noun-এর পরিবর্তে বসে।
* **Verb (V)** → কাজ বা অবস্থা বোঝায়।
* **Adjective (Adj)** → Noun/Pronoun-এর গুণ বোঝায়।
* **Adverb (Adv)** → Verb, Adjective বা অন্য Adverb-কে বর্ণনা করে।
* **Preposition (Prep)** → in, on, at, from, to ইত্যাদি।
* **Conjunction (Conj)** → because, and, but, or ইত্যাদি।
* **Article (Art)** → a, an, the।
* **Interjection (Interj)** → Hi!, Oh!, Wow! ইত্যাদি।
* **Determiner (Det)** → my, your, this, these, some, many ইত্যাদি।
* **Modal Verb** → can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must।
