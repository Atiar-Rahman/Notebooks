## Golden Rule for Exams

**Material Noun = the name of a substance or material from which things are made.** They are generally **uncountable**.

**Examples:**

- Gold → ring
    
- Wood → table
    
- Cotton → shirt
    
- Iron → gate
    
- Glass → window
    
- Paper → book
    
- Leather → shoe
    
- Plastic → bottle
