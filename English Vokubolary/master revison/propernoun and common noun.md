## Quick Revision Trick

**Proper Noun (Specific Name)**

- Rahim
    
- Dhaka
    
- Bangladesh
    
- Padma
    
- Friday
    
- January
    
- Earth
    
- Everest
    

**Common Noun (General Name)**

- boy
    
- girl
    
- city
    
- country
    
- river
    
- mountain
    
- month
    
- day
    
- school
    
- teacher
    

**Golden Rule for the Exam:**

> **Specific name = Proper Noun**  
> **General name = Common Noun**

Practice these 100 MCQs once or twice, and you'll be well prepared for a basic test on Proper Nouns and Common Nouns.

### BCS Level – 10 Hard MCQs

### 1. Identify the sentence in which all the underlined nouns are Proper Nouns.

A. Dhaka is the capital of Bangladesh. B. Padma is a river in Bangladesh. C. Friday is a day of the week. D. Everest is the highest mountain in the world.

✅ Answer: A

Explanation: Dhaka, Bangladesh—both Proper Nouns. In B, “river” is Common; in C, “day” is Common; in D, “mountain” is Common.

### 2. Which one contains both a Proper Noun and a Common Noun?

A. Asia and Europe B. teacher and student C. BUET and university D. Friday and January

✅ Answer: C

Explanation: BUET = Proper, university = Common.

### 3. In the sentence “The Padma flows through Bangladesh.”, which pair is correct?

A. Padma—Common, Bangladesh—Proper B. Padma—Proper, Bangladesh—Proper C. Padma—Proper, Bangladesh—Common D. Padma—Common, Bangladesh—Common

✅ Answer: B

### 4. Which of the following is NOT a Proper Noun?

A. The Daily Star B. Bay of Bengal C. National Parliament House D. newspaper

✅ Answer: D

### 5. Choose the option where the first noun is Common and the second noun is Proper.

A. city — Dhaka B. Bangladesh — country C. Padma — river D. Friday — day

✅ Answer: A

### 6. Which sentence has two Common Nouns and one Proper Noun?

A. Rahim bought a book from a shop. B. Dhaka is a city in Bangladesh. C. Padma is a river in Bangladesh. D. BUET is a university in Dhaka.

✅ Answer: A

Explanation: Rahim (Proper), book (Common), shop (Common).

### 7. Which pair is incorrectly matched?

A. Sundarbans — Proper Noun B. forest — Common Noun C. Royal Bengal Tiger — Common Noun D. Bangladesh — Proper Noun

✅ Answer: C

Explanation: Royal Bengal Tiger is treated as a specific species name and is generally used as a Proper Noun in competitive grammar contexts.

### 8. In “The University of Dhaka is one of the oldest universities in Bangladesh.”, which is the Proper Noun?

A. university B. universities C. Bangladesh D. The University of Dhaka and Bangladesh

✅ Answer: D

### 9. Which option contains only Common Nouns?

A. river, city, teacher, country B. Padma, Dhaka, teacher, country C. Bangladesh, city, river, Friday D. Asia, Europe, continent, country

✅ Answer: A

### 10. BCS-style tricky one:

“The Prime Minister visited the National Martyrs’ Memorial at Savar.” Which noun is Common?

A. Prime Minister B. National Martyrs’ Memorial C. Savar D. Memorial

✅ Answer: A

Explanation: “Prime Minister” is a Common Noun when used as a title without a person’s name. “National Martyrs’ Memorial” and “Savar” are Proper Nouns.
