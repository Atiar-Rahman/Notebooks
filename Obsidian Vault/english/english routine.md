
# 🎯 English Speaking Complete Course

### Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced

## Course Structure

|Level|Lessons|Main Goal|
|---|--:|---|
|🟢 Beginner|1–30|Basic sentence + everyday conversation|

# 🔥 সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ Question-Making Formula

তুমি যেকোনো topic নিয়ে এই **10 ধরনের question** তৈরি করবে:

### 1. What

> What do you like about your job?

### 2. Why

> Why do you like your job?

### 3. Where

> Where do you work?

### 4. When

> When did you start working?

### 5. Who

> Who helped you get your job?

### 6. How

> How did you learn your skills?

### 7. How often

> How often do you practice?

### 8. Have you ever

> Have you ever faced a difficult situation?

### 9. What would

> What would you do if you lost your job?

### 10. What do you think

> What do you think about remote work?

এগুলো দিয়ে **একটি topic-এর উপর 20–30টি question** তৈরি করা সম্ভব।

---

# 🧠 Speaking Answer Formula

শুধু question-এর answer এক sentence-এ শেষ করবে না।

### ❌ Weak

**Q:** What is your hobby?

> My hobby is reading.

### ✅ Better

> My hobby is reading. I usually read books in my free time. I especially like books about technology and personal development. Reading helps me learn new things and improve my thinking.

### 🔥 Advanced

> Reading is one of my favorite hobbies because it allows me to learn new ideas and look at different perspectives. Whenever I have free time, I try to read books related to technology, career development, or personal growth. I believe reading has a long-term impact on both my knowledge and personality.

# ⏱️ Daily 45-Minute Practice

### 10 minutes — Question Making

একটা topic নাও:

**Technology**

তারপর 10–15টা question বানাও।

### 15 minutes — Speaking

প্রতিটি question-এর answer **মুখে বলো**।

লিখে মুখস্থ করবে না।

### 10 minutes — Follow-up

নিজের answer থেকে নতুন question তৈরি করো।

Example:

> I want to become a software engineer.

Follow-up:

> Why do you want to become a software engineer?

> When did you become interested in programming?

> What skills do you need?

> What are you doing to develop those skills?

### 10 minutes — Recording

মোবাইলে নিজের voice record করো।

তারপর দেখো:

- কোথায় থেমে যাচ্ছ?
    
- কোন word বারবার ব্যবহার করছ?
    
- Grammar কোথায় ভুল হচ্ছে?
    
- Pronunciation কেমন?
    
- Sentence কতক্ষণ চালাতে পারছ?

# 🚫 একটা ভুল কখনো করবে না

**English speaking = Grammar মুখস্থ করা না।**

Speaking-এর জন্য তোমার target হবে:

**Think → Question → Answer → Follow-up → Explain → Give Example**

এটা automatic করতে হবে।
